# Narrative Bible

## Overview
A sprawling urban story involving street-level crime, corporate espionage, and factional wars within the fictionalized city.

## Story Structure
- **Act 1**: Introduction to the city, low-level mechanics, acquiring the first safehouse.
- **Act 2**: Expansion into new districts, unlocking high-tier vehicles and weaponry.
- **Act 3**: Climax involving major syndicate takedowns and mastering the full extent of the city.

## Characters
- **Protagonist**: Customizable, rising from the streets to dominate the urban underworld.
- **Allies**: Mechanics, hackers, rogue officers.
- **Factions**:
  - Fictional tech-barons.
  - Smuggling syndicates in the Old City.
  - Corrupt industrial heavyweights.

*(Note: Ensure all organizations and villains are strictly fictional.)*
