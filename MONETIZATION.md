# Monetization Strategy

## Overview
DECCAN HEAT utilizes a balanced, player-friendly mobile monetization approach.

## Revenue Streams
1. **Rewarded Advertising**: Optional ad views for in-game currency, vehicle respawns, or minor heat reduction.
2. **In-App Purchases (IAP)**:
   - Cosmetic items (clothing).
   - High-tier safehouses.
   - Convenience currency packs.
3. **Remove-Ads Purchase**: A premium one-time IAP to remove forced/interstitial ads (if any are introduced) and provide a permanent XP/Currency boost.
4. **Future Content**: Downloadable mission expansions.

## Ethics
- No pay-to-win mechanics in core progression.
- Clear disclosure of drop rates (if any random mechanics are added).
