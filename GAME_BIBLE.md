# Game Bible - DECCAN HEAT

**Working Title**: DECCAN HEAT
**Subtitle**: An Open-World Hyderabad Story
**Genre**: Third-Person Open-World Action/Adventure
**Platforms**: Android & iOS (Mobile-first)

## Product Vision
DECCAN HEAT is a commercial mobile game capturing the broad appeal of modern open-world crime/action sandbox games. Players can freely explore a living city, drive vehicles, interact with NPCs, accept story missions, evade the police, and progress through the world.

## Key Features
- Streamed Open World (4-6 km² total size, split into 6 districts).
- Seamless entry/exit from diverse vehicles (cars, motorcycles, auto-rickshaws).
- 5-Tier Police Heat/Wanted system.
- Economy (Money, Garages, Clothing, Safehouses).
- Immersive Day/Night and Weather systems.

## Target Audience
Mobile gamers seeking a console-like open-world experience optimized for touch devices.

**DISCLAIMER**: The game must remain completely original. No real-world religious communities, ethnic communities, politicians, or identifiable real organizations are to be depicted as villains or in a defamatory manner.
