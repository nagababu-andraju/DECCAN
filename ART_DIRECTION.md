# Art Direction

## Visual Target
- **Style**: Semi-realistic, stylized slightly for mobile performance readability.
- **Palette**: Warm tones, dusty sunlight, neon-lit nights.
- **Inspiration**: The vibrancy, traffic density, and architectural contrasts of Hyderabad.

## Assets and Pipelines
- **Poly Budgets**: Strictly enforced for characters, vehicles, and buildings.
- **LODs**: All 3D assets must have at least 3 levels of detail.
- **Materials**: Merged texture atlases where possible to reduce draw calls. Use URP's Lit and Simple Lit shaders appropriately.
- **Culling**: Aggressive occlusion and frustum culling.
