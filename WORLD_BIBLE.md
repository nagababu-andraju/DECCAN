# World Bible

## Setting
A fictionalized, compressed city inspired by the aesthetics, culture, and architecture of Hyderabad, India. The city is divided into six distinct districts, creating a diverse map of roughly 4-6 km².

## The Six Districts
1. **Old City Bazaars**: Dense historic streets, monuments, bustling markets, tight alleyways.
2. **Central Commercial**: Mid-rises, shopping complexes, theaters, restaurants.
3. **Tech/Business Hub**: Glass facades, wide multi-lane roads, corporate parks, upscale aesthetics.
4. **Affluent Hills**: Expensive estates, winding scenic roads, high vantage points.
5. **Lakefront Urban**: Promenades, mixed commercial/residential, water-side activities.
6. **Peri-Urban/Industrial**: Outer highways, warehouses, factories, garages, and scrap yards.

## Fictional Context
- All business names, criminal organizations, and institutions are purely fictional.
- The geography captures the *vibe* of Hyderabad (rocks, lakes, historic archways, auto-rickshaws) without copying specific real-world trademarked locations.
